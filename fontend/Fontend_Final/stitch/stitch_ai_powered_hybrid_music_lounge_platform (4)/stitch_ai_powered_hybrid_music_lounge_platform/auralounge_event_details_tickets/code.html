About the Artist

Midnight Echoes is the latest project from acclaimed sound architect Elias Thorne. Known for blending organic textures with deep, resonant electronic pulses, Thorne creates environments rather than just songs.

This exclusive performance marks the debut of his new analog-driven setup, designed specifically for the intimate acoustics of The Aura Room. Expect a seamless flow of ambient introspection building to intricate, rhythmic peaks.
